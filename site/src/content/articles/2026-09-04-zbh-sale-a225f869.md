Zimmer Biomet Holdings is a medical technology company that designs, manufactures, and sells orthopedic and surgical products used in joint replacement, spine, sports medicine, and related procedures. Its product lines include implants and instruments for knee, hip, shoulder, and dental reconstruction, as well as tools used in trauma and craniomaxillofacial surgery. The company generates revenue primarily by selling these devices and related surgical instruments to hospitals, surgical centers, and healthcare providers around the world. Zimmer Biomet also invests in research and development to expand its portfolio of implant technologies and digital and robotic-assisted surgical systems, which support its ongoing sales to the healthcare sector. Its customer base spans both domestic and international markets, with revenue tied closely to the volume of orthopedic procedures performed globally.
